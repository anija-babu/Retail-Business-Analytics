# Retail Business Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing retail sales, profitability, customer segments, product performance, and regional trends.

## Dataset

The project uses four datasets:

- Sales Data – Orders, dates, sales, quantity, discounts, profit, shipping cost, and order priority.
- Product Data – Product names, categories, and subcategories.
- Location Data – Cities, states, countries, markets, and regions.
- Customer Data – Customer names and segments.

## Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze retail business performance through three report pages: Sales Analysis, Profit Analysis, and Demographics & Customer Analysis.
## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Analysis
- Total sales and other sales-related KPI cards
- Sales by category and customer segment
- Top-selling products by year
- Sales comparison over time
- Sales by top counties/locations
- Slicers for month and county to explore selected data

### 2. Profit Analysis
- Total profit and profit-related KPI cards
- Profit by category and segment
- Top profitable products
- Profit comparison over time
- Profit by order priority
- Interactive month and county filters

### 3. Demographics and Customer Analysis
- Customer, order, and product overview KPIs
- Top active buyers
- Top valuable customers
- Top profitable states
- Profit distribution by order priority
- Sales and profit by region

## Key Features

- **Interactive filtering:** Use month and county slicers to explore the report.
- **KPI cards:** Review headline business metrics at a glance.
- **Product and customer rankings:** Identify leading products, active buyers, and valuable customers.
- **Trend analysis:** Compare sales and profit over time.
- **Category and segment analysis:** Compare performance across business groups.
- **Regional analysis:** Explore geographic differences in sales and profit.
- **Multi-page navigation:** Move between Sales, Profit, and Demographics pages.

## Tools and Skills

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Microsoft PowerPoint for dashboard planning, layout design, and visual presentation 
- Power Query for data preparation 
- DAX measures and calculations 
- Data visualization and dashboard design
- Business performance analysis

## Business Questions Explored

- Which product categories and customer segments generate the most sales?
- Which products perform best by year?
- How do sales and profit change over time?
- Which counties or regions contribute most to performance?
- Which customers are the most active or valuable?
- How does profitability vary by order priority?
